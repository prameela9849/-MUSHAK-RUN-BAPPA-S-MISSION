HOW TO PLAY

🐘

1. Follow Bappa's Divine Orders

Lord Ganesha gives Mushak sacred orders (Modaks, Diyas, Flags, Flowers, Puja items). Collect the items requested for the current mission!

⌨️

2. Controls & Movement

◀ / A Move Left
▶ / D Move Right
▲ / W / Space Jump over Hurdles
▼ / S Slide under Torans
⚡

3. Divine Power-Ups

🛡️
Vighna Shield: Rare divine orb that protects Mushak against collisions for 30s.
🧲
Modak Magnet: Pulls nearby sacred collectibles towards Mushak for 30s.
⚡
Mushak Rush: Invincible high-speed dash through obstacles for 8s.
✨
Bappa's Blessing: 2x Score Multiplier & golden divine radiance for 12s.
🏆

4. Automatic Best-Score Recording

There is no time limit. Run as far as you can! When a run ends, your score is automatically recorded to your personal best on the Leaderboard.
